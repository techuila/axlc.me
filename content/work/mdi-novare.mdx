## About the company

MDI Novare (now NOVARE) is one of the Philippines' largest technology
consultancies, driving digital transformation for enterprises across
software development, cloud, and data. I consulted through them on Meralco's
Bayad Center — one of the country's biggest bills-payment platforms.

## My journey there

I led the BFA module team on the Bayad Center project: technical design,
backend development, testing strategy, and delivery. The engagement was
renewed across successive contracts through final handover.

## Notable contributions

- Designed and shipped backend features on AWS from project requirements.
- Built integration tests per feature, strengthening regression protection.
- Mentored developers via code reviews and engineering standards.
- Ran knowledge-transfer sessions through final handover; retained across
  successive contract renewals.
