## About the company

Monark (previously Viyahe) builds trials.gg, a challenge-based reward system
for competitive gamers: players complete in-game challenges, progress is
computed from a stream of gameplay events, and rewards are paid out on
completion. Wrong progress means wrong rewards — correctness is a direct
trust and payout problem. The team was small, remote, and shipped about five
times a week.

## My journey there

I joined early as a core engineer and stayed through production launch,
owning the backend and cloud side end to end: initial architecture, delivery,
CI/CD, observability, and the operations after launch. From 2023–2024 I also
supported frontend delivery when the team needed it.

## Notable contributions

- Led end-to-end delivery (architecture → production); 900+ sign-ups,
  ~5 hrs/day average engagement, 99.9% availability.
- Diagnosed and removed a race condition that silently lost player progress,
  restructuring the event pipeline around per-player ordering with
  idempotent, transactional handlers.
- Designed the AWS data-analytics pipeline (Firehose → Glue → Athena) with
  near-real-time Grafana dashboards.
- Owned CI/CD on AWS CodePipeline across frontend and backend — ~5
  deploys/week.
- Shipped 35+ web/Electron features while supporting frontend delivery.
- Enforced clean-code architecture and team-wide standards.
